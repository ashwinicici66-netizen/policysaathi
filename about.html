About PolicySaathi

Why this site exists

Insurance paperwork rarely gives a straight answer. “What will I get if I surrender?” is a question neither agents nor brochures answer clearly.

PolicySaathi has one goal: show what the law already says, in plain language. No paperwork, no forms, no phone calls.

Where the numbers come from

  • GSV percentages — minimums set by the IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024
  • Paid-up formula — IRDAI’s reduced paid-up provisions
  • Chart estimates — calculated within those rules, with clear disclaimers

Our job is to give you an estimate so you can ask the right questions. The final figure always comes from your insurer.

What we are not

  • We are not tied to any insurance company, agent or broker
  • We don’t sell policies, take commissions, or collect your phone number
  • Our calculator collects no personal data — everything runs in your browser
Spotted an error or have a question? Write to us via the contact page — every message is read.
