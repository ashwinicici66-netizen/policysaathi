The rules below come from IRDAI and apply to every life insurer in India — LIC, HDFC Life, ICICI Prudential, SBI Life and others. “LIC” is just the name most people search for.

Minimum GSV table (IRDAI 2024)

If you surrender a life insurance policy with regular or limited premiums, the insurer must pay at least this percentage of the premiums you have paid:

Policy year (when surrendered)Minimum GSV
2nd year30% of total premiums paid
3rd year35% of total premiums paid
4th to 7th year50% of total premiums paid
After the 7th yearRises progressively, up to 90%
Final 2 policy yearsAt least 90% of total premiums paid
Formula: Surrender value = Total premiums paid × applicable GSV %. The insurer may pay more (special surrender value) — never less.

Worked example

Suppose you pay ₹50,000 a year, have completed 5 years (total ₹2,50,000), and the policy term is 15 years. The 5th year falls in the 4–7 year bracket → 50%.

Minimum surrender value = ₹2,50,000 × 50% = ₹1,25,000. In other words, ₹1,25,000 of your premiums does not come back — which is why surrendering should be a last resort.

How to read your result

  • Estimated surrender value — the legal minimum. The actual amount is often higher.
  • Paid-up row — if you leave the policy paid-up instead of surrendering, this is the reduced sum assured you would get at maturity (not now).
  • ULIP — after 5 years, surrender value equals the fund value (units × NAV), which depends on the market. That is why the calculator shows only a note for ULIPs.

What you need to surrender

  1. Surrender form (from the insurer’s website or branch)
  2. Original policy document
  3. Bank account details (cancelled cheque or passbook)
  4. KYC — PAN and Aadhaar

Money usually arrives in 7–15 working days. Note: after surrender, your life cover ends, and buying new cover later will cost more at an older age.

Calculator questions

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The entire calculation runs in your browser — no premium or sum assured ever reaches our server. The calculator works even without an internet connection once the page has loaded.

My policy is from LIC — does this table apply?

Yes. IRDAI rules apply to every life insurer, including LIC. However, policies bought before October 2024 may follow older, product-specific surrender rules — check the surrender value table in your policy document.

Can I surrender term insurance?

Pure term plans generally have no surrender value, because there is no savings component — the premium pays for the life cover.

Is the calculation different for money-back policies?

Yes. If you have already received survival benefits, those are deducted from the GSV. In that case the calculator shows an upper-bound estimate — get the exact figure from your insurer.
