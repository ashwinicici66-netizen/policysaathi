Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to receive the money after surrendering?

Usually 7–15 working days. You submit the surrender form, original policy document, bank details and KYC. Some insurers accept surrender online and pay directly into your bank account.

Can I surrender before 2 years?

For regular premium policies, there is no surrender value until premiums for 2 years are paid — you get nothing. Under IRDAI’s 2024 rules, some policies may qualify for a special surrender value after 1 year. Check your policy document or ask your insurer.

What is the difference between GSV and SSV?

GSV (Guaranteed Surrender Value) is the legal minimum, which this calculator shows. SSV (Special Surrender Value) is the insurer’s own calculation and is usually higher. You receive whichever is higher.

Should I surrender or leave the policy paid-up?

If you need money now, surrender. If not, paid-up is usually better — the policy stays alive, pays at maturity, and keeps a life cover. Compare both in our guide.

Is this calculator exact?

No — it is an estimate based on IRDAI’s minimum rules. The exact amount depends on your insurer’s illustration, bonuses and policy terms. Confirm the final figure with your insurer’s portal or branch.

Does this apply to car or bike insurance?

No. Vehicle insurance (car, bike) has no surrender value. If you sell the vehicle, you can cancel the policy and the insurer refunds the premium for the unused period, minus a small cancellation charge. Health insurance works similarly — it runs for the policy year and premiums are not refunded on cancellation. This calculator is only for life insurance policies.
