LIC surrender value chart

The guaranteed surrender value percentage for every policy year — at a glance.

First, the important part: These percentages come from IRDAI’s minimum GSV rules, which apply to LIC and every other life insurer in India. Your policy’s actual chart is printed in the “Surrender Value” section of the policy document — that is always final.

Is this chart only for LIC?

No. The same rules apply to HDFC Life, ICICI Prudential, SBI Life, Max Life, Bajaj Allianz Life and every other life insurer. “LIC” is simply the name people search most often. This chart is not for car, bike or health insurance — those policies have no surrender value.

Basic table — for any life policy

Older policies (bought before October 2024)

Policies issued before the 2024 regulations follow their product’s original surrender rules — many endowment plans start at 30% after 3 years. So:

  • Open the “Surrender Value” page of your policy document
  • Or ask your insurer for a surrender quote
  • Treat this calculator’s number as a rough estimate — a starting point, not the final figure

Chart questions

How do I check my LIC surrender value online?

Log in to LIC’s customer portal (licindia.in) with your policy details. Or ask the branch for a “surrender quotation” — it is free.

The percentage is of which premium?

Of the total premiums paid so far (excluding taxes and extra premiums). For example, 5 years at ₹50,000 = ₹2,50,000; 50% of that is ₹1,25,000.

Why is bonus not shown in the chart?

GSV applies only to premiums. Bonus value is added in the SSV (special surrender value) — which is why the actual payout is usually higher than the chart.

Does a car or bike insurance policy have a surrender chart?

No. Vehicle insurance has no surrender value. If you cancel the policy, the insurer refunds the premium for the remaining period minus a small cancellation charge. This chart is only for life insurance.
