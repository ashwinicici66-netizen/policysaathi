Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The calculator collects no data

Whatever you enter in the calculator — premium, sum assured, years — stays only in your browser. We have no server that stores it and no login system. Close the page and it is gone.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies itself. However, third-party services (below) may use cookies:

  • Google Fonts — to load fonts
  • Google AdSense — to display ads (once advertising is enabled)

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may display Google AdSense ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • About Google’s advertising cookies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • To opt out of personalised ads: adssettings.google.com
  • Third-party cookie choices across sites: aboutads.info/choices

Server logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited), as with any website. We do not link these logs to any personal identity.

Email

If you email us, that email stays with us. We never sell your email to third parties. You don’t need to send your policy number, premium or other personal details.

Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, this page will be updated with a new date.

Questions

For any privacy question, write via the contact page.
